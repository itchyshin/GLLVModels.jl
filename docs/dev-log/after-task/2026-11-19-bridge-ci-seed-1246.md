# After-task: bridge_fit ci_seed validation (#1246)

**Date:** 2026-11-19. **Branch:** `cursor/bridge-ci-seed-check-1246-8da0`. **Issue:** #1246 (refs only).

## Goal

A non-integer `ci_seed` stops with an `ArgumentError` that names `bridge_fit` and the option, instead of a raw `InexactError` or `MethodError`.

## Change

- `src/bridge.jl`: `_bridge_ci_seed` checks `Real`, finite, integral and in `Int` range before `Int(...)`, same style as `_bridge_ci_nboot`; the options list in the `bridge_fit` docstring says the seed is an integer.
- `test/test_bridge_ci_nboot.jl`: new testset (refused: 2.5, NaN, Inf, "a", 1e30, nothing, for bootstrap, wald and none; accepted: 1 and 1.0 give identical intervals).

## Evidence

See `docs/dev-log/check-log.md` entry of 2026-11-19. New testset 42/42 on the fix; 3 passed, 19 failed, 20 errored on the old source.

## Not done / limits

- A negative seed is accepted by the check and still fails later with a `DomainError` from `MersenneTwister` on the bootstrap route; making it an error up front would also refuse `ci_method = "wald"` calls that work today, so it is left as is.
- `ci_level` is not touched (the issue found it already errors downstream).
- Full `Pkg.test()`, JET and Aqua were not run. No tolerance or expectation was changed. Rose verdict: not run.
