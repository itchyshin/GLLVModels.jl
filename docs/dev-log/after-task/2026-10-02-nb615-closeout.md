## 1. Goal

Make per-species NB2 fits converge on the #615 grid, and settle the default NB grouping for `fit_gllvm`.

## 2. Implemented

- #655: `_nb_poisson_ridge_polish`. A non-converged per-species fit with species at r > 1e3 is refit with those species fixed at r = 1e10. The refit is kept only if it is no worse.
- #659: up to three more polish rounds, re-fixing species above 1e3 each round, with a plain continuation as the fallback.
- #664: the polish refits use dense BFGS (keyword `alg`) instead of L-BFGS. Merged with #658's exact `grad` path, whose BFGS is identical.
- #665: bare `fit_gllvm(Y; family = NegativeBinomial())` fits one shared r (`fit_nb_gllvm`). `disp_group = :species` gives per-species r. `gllvm(@formula(...))` and the R bridge keep per-trait r. Parity tests and tools pass `disp_group = :species`, and the docs are updated.
- Grid evidence (Totoro): per-species convergence went from 1,051 of 1,440 to 1,440 of 1,440. #615 is closed.

## 3a. Decisions and Rejected Alternatives

- Maintainer, 2026-10-01: "shared r default", scoped to `fit_gllvm` only. Two alternatives were offered: "everywhere" (it would break the default parity with gllvmTMB on the bridge) and keeping per-species with docs.
- Rejected a larger iteration cap for the first fit: on the ridge it crawls to the cap, and more iterations only waste time.
- Rejected warm starts from the shared fit: same optima, no speed-up (#615 comment, 2026-10-01).
- Rejected more L-BFGS rounds as the final fix. From saved end points, BFGS converged on 17 of 17, against 7 of 17 for L-BFGS.
- Left the hash-pinned parity contracts unedited. The true-parity lane added a dated note in #666.
