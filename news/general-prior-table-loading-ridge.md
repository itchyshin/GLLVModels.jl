- **Binomial covariate fits accept named Normal and Student-t priors on shared coefficients and response-specific intercepts, with an optional loading ridge.** Supply `response_names` in the row order of a wide response matrix; long-format formula fits use their sorted species levels. The prior-fit `confint` route refuses uncalibrated penalized curvature.
